Wyze Customer Frustration Recovery Playbook

  • Empathy First: "I completely understand how frustrating it is when your camera drops connection. Let's get this sorted out right now."
  • Set Expectations: "I am looking up your subscription right now—this will take about 1 minute."

Welcome to Wyze - ECE Chat Simulator

A quick guide to the controls:

  • Theme Switcher: choose between Wyze Teal, Dark Cockpit, or Warm Classic at the top.
  • Scenario and mood: choose the customer’s issue and tone before starting, or click a quick starter.
  • Your avatar: the character you chose appears beside your replies. With 2 chats on, use the Chat 1 / Chat 2 tabs to switch conversations; Timer shows reply time.
  • Start new customer: create a new practice conversation.
  • Send / Clear chat: reply to the customer; clearing archives the transcript and requires a new customer.
  • Coaching notes / End conversation: request feedback during practice or finish the drill for a QA review.
  • Case file: open or close your notes. Use Copy transcript & notes to copy them.
  • My Performance: view your personal typing speed, response latency, abandonment analysis, and AI insights.
  • Trainer view: reveal the answer key and ideal SOP resolution.
  • History: review conversations. Enter the trainer passcode and press Unlock to monitor live chats; Lock hides live sessions again. Expand the window for class review. In a transcript, Back returns to the list and Export locally downloads it.
  • ? reopen the full help guide. Retry appears if an action fails.

How this works

  1. Start a new customer. Optionally set a scenario type and mood, choose a theme, or leave both random.
  2. Handle the chat the way you would on the floor — apply your process, ask verification questions, and troubleshoot. The customer responds to your questions and follows reasonable instructions. If you go quiet for three minutes or run past a hold time you promised, the customer may check back in. If you remain quiet for 7 minutes, the customer abandons the chat.
  3. Log the case in the fields on the right as you learn each detail. Nothing is pre-filled on purpose — that's the practice.
  4. Get coaching notes any time after the first reply for a read on what you did well and what to tighten up. The coach remembers your past sessions so it can track your consistency and improvement.
  5. Watch your typing speed — every reply you type is timed (words per minute) so you can see whether your responses are fast enough for a live-chat queue.
  6. Track your performance: click 📊 My Performance to review your typing speed distribution, response times, frustration recovery rate, and AI coaching insights.
  7. Trainer view reveals the real persona details and ideal resolution, for checking a trainee's notes against the truth.
  8. History keeps recent conversations for seven days. Enter the trainer passcode to review live chats and expand the window for class review.

Your case notes and recent conversations are saved for seven days for coaching review. Never paste real customer information into this tool.
